You won't find Minecraft for free (but here's what people actually mean)

Short version: the full game, Java or Bedrock, costs money. The free official stuff is Minecraft Classic in your browser, the Java demo, and trials on some platforms. Cracked launchers and Eaglercraft exist, but they can only join servers that switched off account checks, and almost every big server hasn't.

If you typed "minecraft free" into a search bar, you were probably hoping for a download button. There isn't one. Minecraft has been a paid game since 2009 and that's not changing.

What does exist is a handful of official freebies, some sketchy launchers, and a whole browser scene that technically runs Minecraft but can't get you onto most servers. Here's what each of those actually gets you.

The free stuff that's actually legit

Minecraft Classic

Mojang put the 2009 version up at classic.minecraft.net for the game's 10th birthday. It runs in your browser, no account, no download. You get creative mode, a small pile of blocks, and an invite link so a few friends can build with you.

It doesn't save once you close the tab, and there's no survival, no mobs, no Nether. It's a museum piece. Fun for ten minutes, and it's the only version that's free forever.

The Java demo

Make a Microsoft account, install the official launcher, and you can play the demo without buying anything. It's one world with a time limit, roughly five in-game days. That's enough to find out if your PC can run it before you spend money, which is honestly the best use of it.

Trials on other platforms

Some devices get a Bedrock trial. Which ones changes, so check minecraft.net/free-trial instead of trusting a random blog (including this one, a year from now).

Minecraft Education

If your school pays for the right Microsoft 365 plan, you might already have this through your school login. It's its own version made for classrooms. It's real Minecraft with lessons and teacher tools bolted on, but it's walled off from the normal server list. You're not getting onto Hypixel with it.

Game Pass

Not free, but worth checking. PC Game Pass includes Java and Bedrock. If someone in your house already pays for it, you might already "own" Minecraft without knowing.

So what is a cracked launcher actually doing?

This is the part people get mixed up about.

When you buy Minecraft, you're really buying an account. The game files themselves get downloaded from Mojang's servers by whatever launcher you use. A launcher's whole job is: log you in, grab the right version, start the game.

A cracked (or "offline") launcher just skips the login part. You type any name you want, and it starts the game in offline mode. Singleplayer works fine. Then you try to join a server and get this:

Failed to verify username!

That's the server asking Mojang "hey, does this person own the game?" and Mojang going "never heard of them." Any server with online-mode=true in its settings does this check, and that's the default. Hypixel does it. Basically every big network does it. Most friend servers do it without the owner even thinking about it.

Where cracked players can actually play

Servers that turned online mode off. People call these cracked servers, and a lot of anarchy servers run this way. They exist and some are pretty big, but that's your whole pool. The normal server list is off limits.

It also comes with a weird side effect. With no account check, anyone can join as anyone. Your username isn't really yours, someone else can log in as you. Most offline servers patch this with a /register and /login password plugin, which works, kind of.

Skins are another one. Your skin is tied to your account, so no account means everyone sees you as Steve or Alex unless the server runs a skin plugin.

The launchers themselves

Some of the popular cracked launchers have had a bad reputation for years over bundled junk and shady behavior. I'm not linking any of them. If a download asks you to turn off your antivirus first, that's your answer right there.

Legit third-party launchers like Prism are a different thing. They're great, but they want you signed in with an account that owns the game.

And then there's Eaglercraft

Eaglercraft is old Minecraft (1.8.8 mostly, plus 1.5.2 and a 1.12 build) recompiled to run in a browser tab. That's why it's all over "minecraft unblocked" searches and school Chromebooks. It's honestly impressive.

Same wall, though. It can't sign into a Microsoft account, and it connects over a different kind of connection than the normal game does. A server has to install a specific plugin to let Eaglercraft players in at all. So you're playing on Eaglercraft servers, not the regular server list. Mojang has also sent a pile of takedowns at it, which is why the links keep dying and you end up on mirror sites covered in ads.

I wrote up the whole thing here: what Eaglercraft is and why it can't join normal servers.

Is it worth just buying it?

Yeah. On PC, Java and Bedrock come bundled in one purchase now, it goes on sale, and people have gotten more than a decade out of one copy. You get the full server list, Realms, your actual skin, and mods through normal launchers.

If you just want to make something Minecraft-y without owning the game, Classic is right there. Or, shameless plug, the skin editor and the pixel art generator on this site work without owning anything. You'll just want the real game eventually to use what you make.

Already bought it but not sure which version to play? Java vs Bedrock is the next thing to figure out.

Questions people ask

Is Minecraft free on PC?

No. Java and Bedrock for PC is a one-time purchase that includes both versions. The free official options are Minecraft Classic in the browser, the Java demo, and trials on some platforms.

Is there a version of Minecraft that's free forever?

Minecraft Classic at classic.minecraft.net. It's the 2009 creative mode in your browser. It doesn't save and has no survival, but it's free and official.

Why does a server say Failed to verify username?

The server asked Mojang whether your account owns the game and Mojang said no. Cracked and offline launchers have no real account, so any server with online mode turned on, which is the default, kicks them.

Can a cracked launcher join Hypixel?

No. Hypixel and almost every big network run in online mode, so they check your account with Mojang. Cracked players can only join servers that turned that check off.

Is Eaglercraft free Minecraft?

Eaglercraft is old Minecraft Java ported to the browser without Mojang's permission. It runs for free, but it can only join Eaglercraft servers, not normal ones, and Mojang has been sending takedowns at it.
